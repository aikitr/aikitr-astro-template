## Data collected by this template

The contact form receives a name, email address and message. The subscribe form receives an email address and records consent. A Cloudflare Worker writes this information to the site owner's Supabase project. This starter does not expose submissions to the browser or send email automatically.

## Who manages the data

The operator of the generated site configures Supabase, controls access, sets retention periods and handles requests about the data. This template does not provide a shared data management service.

## Before publishing

State the actual operator, contact method, purposes, retention period and deletion process. Describe any analytics, anti-abuse or email services you enable. Update this page if you change the form fields.
